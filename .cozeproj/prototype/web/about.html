About Chengfeng International

Crafting premium men's knitwear since 2008

Our Story

Founded in 2008, Chengfeng International has grown from a small workshop in Guangdong to a trusted manufacturing partner for over 500 retail brands worldwide. We specialize in premium men's knitwear, combining traditional craftsmanship with modern production efficiency.

Our dedication to quality is woven into every garment — from yarn selection to final stitch. We believe that great knitwear begins with understanding the needs of the brands that wear our name behind their labels.

Today, our state-of-the-art facility operates three full production lines, staffed by over 200 skilled workers who share a single commitment: delivering excellence on time, every time.
